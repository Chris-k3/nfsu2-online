# Free-roam multiplayer: the friend of roam_mp_park.txt, a stage-4 driver on
# autopilot that watches: mp status every 2 s (the friend's status, and who
# is shown, hidden or parked), ${ROAM_STEPS} steps, then quit.
0     roam fe2roam 14000
+0    roam smsauto on
+0    log ROAM_INROAM
+0    roam mp waitpeers 1 9000
+240  roam call 0x5fae20 ecx=*0x8900ac          # autopilot on
+0    loop ${ROAM_STEPS}
+2000ms roam mp status
+0    endloop
+0    status
+0    roam call 0x5faf10 ecx=*0x8900ac          # autopilot off
+10   quit
