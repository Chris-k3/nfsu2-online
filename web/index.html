    Changes from the original

    • The PC game's own code, recompiled to run in Chrome and Firefox, with WebGPU for the graphics and Web Audio for the sound.
    • Up to 120 fps, widescreen at your screen's resolution.
    • The original look, with the game's graphics settings at their highest.
    • Startup movies skip with one press.
    • Free roam and Online mode with friends on this server, no EA account needed.
    • A horn to honk at the other players, in free roam and in races.
    • Xbox and PlayStation controllers, with rumble.
    • The game pauses when its tab is in the background.
    • Autosave for every profile, and a note each time the game saves.
    • Saves stay in this browser, with zip backups.

    Saves

      Quit from the game's main menu, or F9 then Quit game, so a save in progress is never cut short.
